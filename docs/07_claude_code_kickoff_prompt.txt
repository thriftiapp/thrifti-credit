- Always written "thrifti", all lowercase, everywhere. Never "Thrifti".
- Research-stage idea in Canada. Not incorporated. Not a lender.
- Problem: many Canadians with lower credit scores pay 20 percent or more on
  credit cards even when they pay rent and bills on time. Low scores often come
