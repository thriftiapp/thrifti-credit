Questions

The short version

Without the fine print.

Is this a loan application?

No. thrifti does not lend money or check credit today. Joining the research list helps us build this with real people instead of assumptions.

Will you check my credit?

Not to join the list. We ask only for your name, email and province, and nothing is sent to a credit bureau. If a pilot ever runs, the lending partner would explain any checks before anything happens.

Does joining affect my credit score?

No. There is no credit check, no hard inquiry, and no contact with any bureau.

How would thrifti see my habits without spying on me?

Through a read-only connection you approve and can turn off, used only to check specific evidence: bills paid on time, savings streaks, spending under income. No data is sold, and the decision log shows you exactly what was read, like the examples above.

Are the rates on this page real offers?

No. The 9.4% rate and the habit discounts are worked examples to show how habit-based pricing could work. Real rates would be set by a licensed lending partner.

Will this become a product?

Only if the research shows it really helps, and only with a licensed lending partner. There is no launch date. People on the research list hear first.
