Privacy

thrifti is a research project. This page explains what we collect when you join the waitlist, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your first name
  • Your email address
  • Your province or territory
  • Whether you would like to be invited to a 30-minute interview

We do not ask for your credit score, bank details or any financial information.

Why we collect it

  • To send you updates about the waitlist
  • To invite you to an interview, if you said yes

Where it is stored

The waitlist form is run by Tally (tally.so). Your answers are stored by Tally on our behalf. Tally may store information on servers outside Canada, where it may be subject to the laws of that country. This website does not store any of your information.

What we will not do

We will never sell your information.

Your choices

You can ask us to see or delete your information at any time. Email contact@thrifti.app and we will remove it.

Our approach

We follow the principles of Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Who we are

thrifti, a research project based in Canada.
