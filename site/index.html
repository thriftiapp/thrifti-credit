The problem

Paying your bills should count for something

One bad month lasts for years

A job loss or a medical bill can lower your credit score for a long time, even after life is back on track.

A lower score costs more

A lower score usually means higher interest. That makes debt harder to pay off, even when you pay every month.

Rent often does not count

Credit scores often ignore years of paying rent, utilities and your phone bill on time.

How it would work

A score you can understand

The idea is a transparent score, called the CARE Score, built from five things you can see and control.

  1. 1

    Income stability

    Is money coming in regularly?

  2. 2

    Steady spending

    Are your regular costs predictable?

  3. 3

    Cash flow cushion

    Is there some room left after bills?

  4. 4

    Rent and bills paid

    Do you pay rent, utilities and your phone on time?

  5. 5

    Staying in touch

    Do you keep in contact when things get hard?

Questions

Good to know

Is this a loan application?

No. thrifti does not lend money today. Joining the waitlist is not an application.

Will you check my credit?

No. We only collect your name, email and province. Two questions about your debt and bills are optional and ask for ranges, not exact amounts.

Does joining affect my credit score?

No. We do not contact any credit bureau.

What happens to my information?

It is stored by our form provider, Tally, and never sold. You can ask us to delete it at any time. See our privacy page.

Who is behind thrifti?

A small team with long experience in finance and financial inclusion, based in Ontario. You can reach us at contact@thrifti.app.
