Credit that looks at how you actually manage money

Millions of Canadians pay 20 percent or more on credit cards, even when they pay rent and bills on time. We are exploring a fairer way to consolidate debt, based on your real financial habits, not just a credit score.

The problem

  • A single bad month, like a job loss or a medical bill, can lower your credit score for years.
  • A lower score usually means higher interest, which makes debt harder to pay off.
  • Credit scores often ignore things like paying rent and utilities on time.

The idea

  • Look at stable income and on-time payments for rent and essential bills.
  • Offer one consolidation option at a fair rate, through a licensed lending partner.
  • Show clearly how every decision is made, with no hidden fees.

Where things stand

thrifti is an idea at the research stage. We do not offer loans or credit today, and joining the waitlist is not an application. We are talking to people first to learn whether this would really help.

Help us shape it

We are looking for people in Canada who carry credit card or other high-interest debt for a 30-minute conversation. Participants receive a $25 gift card as thanks.
