thrifti credit: architecture and cost to go live

Draft for AD, 12 November 2026. Vendor prices were checked on each vendor's own pricing page today and are in USD unless the vendor says otherwise. Items without a published price are marked "quote needed" and left blank, not guessed.

1. Architecture in one picture

App (what customers use): mobile web app that installs from the browser (no app store). Hosted on Cloudflare, same as credit.thrifti.app today.
API layer: Cloudflare Workers. Checks who is signed in, records consent, passes requests on. Stores no raw data itself.
Database, sign-in and file storage: one managed Postgres service with built-in sign-in and file storage (Supabase is the candidate). Must be hosted in Canada; the Canada region is not confirmed on their pricing page and must be checked before any real data.
Bank linking: Flinks (Canadian), read-only. Bank data goes straight into our database.
Documents: customer uploads credit report, CRA notice, rent and bill proofs. A reader pulls the numbers out; a person checks them.
Scoring service: the CARE points model from the research project, in Python. Runs as a small separate service; every result saved with its reasons and model version.
Reviewer console: staff-only screen. AI prepares, a person decides, the note is required, every action logged.
Partner hand-off: approved files go to the credit union by a secure transfer they choose. Funds go from them to the customer's cards.
Messages: sign-in codes by SMS (Twilio), email from contact@thrifti.app (Zoho, already in place).
Records that must never be lost: consent log, decision log, audit log. Kept apart and never edited, only added to.
